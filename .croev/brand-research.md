# Fundsmart AI — Brand Research

## What they do
Independent UK credit broker. Connects SMEs with FCA-regulated lenders via AI-powered matching. Positions on **speed, transparency, and consumer protection**.

## Target audience
- UK-based small-to-medium enterprises
- Annual turnover > £200k
- Trading 1+ years
- Owners who want capital fast without lengthy bank processes

## Core messaging (from /landinggov)
- **H1:** "Stop Guessing Know Your Options"
- **Sub:** "Fund smart. Faster. Clearer. Safer."

## Offer / Mechanism
- 2-minute pre-qualification
- Soft credit search only (no credit score impact)
- Funding in 6–48 hours
- 100% free to the borrower (commission from lenders)
- Human specialist support

## Proof points
- 142,468 UK businesses served
- "Trusted by 100,000+ UK SMEs"
- 93% approval rate
- FCA-regulated lender partners: Iwoca, Funding Circle, Halo (+ others)
- Case studies: £50k–£250k secured in 24h–3 days

## CTAs observed
- "Get Started"
- Amount selector (£10k → £1m+)
- "See your offers instantly"

## UVPs
- Single best-match lender (not shotgun mass application)
- Data privacy: "never sold"
- Soft-credit-only assessment
- Supports previously declined applicants

## Visual identity (rough, from page)
- Accent: teal / turquoise
- Background: white / light
- Text: dark
